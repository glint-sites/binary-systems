About Binary System Medical

Advancing diagnostics in Saudi Arabia since 2015.

Binary System Medical Co. is a Saudi-based medical technology company and a subsidiary of Saudi Mais Co. Certified by the Saudi Food and Drug Authority (SFDA), we specialise in In Vitro Diagnostics (IVD) and Medical Devices (MD) solutions.

AWho we are

Our role goes beyond supply.

Since 2015, we have supported hospitals, laboratories, universities and research centres with advanced solutions sourced from trusted global manufacturers.

We provide expert consultation, proper system selection and long-term support — so every technology we bring to the Kingdom is matched to the people who use it, and keeps performing long after installation.

As Saudi Arabia continues to invest in world-class healthcare, we help providers adopt diagnostic technology that meets international standards and SFDA requirements.
