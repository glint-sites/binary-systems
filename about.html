Diagnostics and medical technology in Saudi Arabia since 2015

Binary System Medical Co. is a Saudi-based medical technology company and a subsidiary of Saudi Mais Co. Certified by the Saudi Food and Drug Authority (SFDA), we specialise in In Vitro Diagnostics (IVD) and Medical Devices (MD) solutions.

Who we are

Since 2015 we have supplied hospitals, laboratories, universities and research centres with diagnostic systems and medical devices from manufacturers in Europe, Asia and North America.

We provide expert consultation, careful system selection and long-term support, so every technology we bring to the Kingdom suits the people who use it and keeps performing long after installation.
