Privacy policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy explains how Binary System Medical Co. ("BSM", "we") handles personal information collected through binarysys.net.

Information we collect

We only collect the information you choose to send us through our contact and quotation forms: your name, work email, phone number, organisation, job title, facility type, city, the products in your quote list and your message.

How we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry or prepare a quotation.
  • To follow up about the products or services you asked about.
  • To keep a record of business correspondence.

We do not sell your information or use it for unrelated marketing.

Form delivery and spam protection

Form submissions are processed by our website host (Cloudflare) and delivered to our team by email or a secure business system. To block automated spam, our forms use ALTCHA, a privacy-friendly check that runs a small calculation in your browser. It does not use cookies or track you, and it is served from our own website.

Your browser

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. A few things are stored only in your own browser (local storage) so they stay with you while you browse or switch language: your quote list, your language choice and any form you have started but not sent. Nothing is sent to us unless you submit a form. Unsent form entries are deleted after 12 hours or as soon as you send the form, and you can clear everything at any time by clearing your browser data.

Retention and security

We keep enquiry information only for as long as needed to respond and to maintain business records, and we protect it with appropriate technical and organisational measures.

Your rights

In line with Saudi Arabia's Personal Data Protection Law, you may ask to access, correct or delete the personal information you have sent us. Contact us at info@binarysys.net.
