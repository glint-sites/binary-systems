About DIANA Molecular Reagents & Discovery

DIANA Biotechnologies is a Czech biotechnology company founded in 2018 as a spin-off from the Institute of Organic Chemistry and Biochemistry (IOCB) in Prague, built around its proprietary DIANA (DNA-linked Inhibitor Antibody Assay) technology.

It works across four areas — molecular reagents, antibodies, diagnostics and drug discovery — supplying PCR and RT-PCR mixes, reverse transcriptases and RNase inhibitors, developing monoclonal antibodies, and offering kinase-inhibitor profiling. An ultrasensitive qPCR sepsis assay is in development.
