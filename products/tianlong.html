About Tianlong Molecular Systems

Established in 1997 in Xi'an, Tianlong is a pioneer in nucleic-acid testing instruments and reagents, with products marketed in more than 100 countries.

Its portfolio covers the complete molecular workflow: GeneRotex extractors, Gentier real-time PCR systems and PANA/Panall automated workstations, together with 600+ compatible kits for extraction and detection.

Key highlights

  • GeneRotex 96: 1–96 samples per run with rotary mixing
  • Gentier 96E: 96 wells, six channels, plate scanned in 7 seconds
  • Gentier X3: three independent 32-well blocks in parallel
  • PANA 3200S workstation: 32 samples, extraction to PCR
  • 600+ compatible extraction and detection kits
