About Pannoramic Digital Pathology

3DHISTECH provides clinical pathologists, researchers and universities with digital pathology solutions that support fast, accurate diagnostics, medical discovery and education.

The Pannoramic range spans diagnostic DX scanners (such as the Pannoramic 1000 DX for high-volume laboratories) and research scanners with brightfield and fluorescence imaging, supported by 3DHISTECH case management, viewing and analysis software.

Key highlights

  • Pannoramic 1000 DX: up to 1,200 slides, up to 80 slides/hour
  • Pannoramic 250 Flash III DX: 300-slide capacity
  • Research models add multi-channel fluorescence imaging
  • HL7 LIS/HIS integration with MRXS or DICOM output
  • DX scanners described by 3DHISTECH as IVDR-compliant
