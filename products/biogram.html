About Bio-Gram ColorJet NANO Staining

Founded in 2019, Bio-Gram Diagnostics develops in vitro diagnostic solutions for smear staining and sample preparation in haematology and microbiology.

The compact ColorJet NANO uses Flow-Stream-Staining Technology (FS²T) to stain up to six slides at once, while the JetDye-AF range provides alcohol-free, ready-to-use Gram, Wright, Giemsa and May-Grünwald stains — helping laboratories standardise staining quality.

  • 01ColorJet NANO: up to 60 slides per hour
