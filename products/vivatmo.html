About Vivatmo pro & Vivatmo me

The biomarker FeNO (fractional exhaled nitric oxide) can significantly improve the diagnosis and therapy of asthma by giving an objective measure of airway inflammation.

Bosch's Vivatmo system measures FeNO in exhaled breath simply and precisely: Vivatmo pro is designed for practices and clinics, while Vivatmo me lets patients monitor at home with a companion app. Disposable Oxycap mouthpieces include a germ filter.

BKey highlights

  • 01Vivatmo pro: about 10-second measurement for adults, 6 seconds for children
  • 02No sensor replacement or recalibration needed
  • 03Vivatmo me home device syncs with the Vivatmo app via Bluetooth
  • 04Disposable Oxycap mouthpiece with integrated germ filter
  • 05Vivatmo pro connects via Wi-Fi, USB, Ethernet, HL7 and GDT
