About GeneProof PCR Kits

GeneProof, based in Brno, develops real-time PCR kits for the molecular diagnosis of serious infections and genetic conditions. Since 2022 it has been part of NuvinkaDx, a diagnostics group that also includes ALPCO.

Its portfolio of more than 80 CE-IVD real-time PCR products spans bloodborne, transplant, sexually transmitted, respiratory and neurological infections and thrombotic mutations, complemented by extraction systems and the myCROBE™ automated platform.
