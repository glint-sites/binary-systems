Why regular STI testing is essential for your health

April 22, 20262 min readBy the BSM team

Many sexually transmitted infections show no symptoms in their early stages. Regular screening, increasingly with PCR-based molecular tests, is an essential part of preventive care.

Early detection saves lives

Sexually transmitted infections (STIs) are among the most common infectious diseases worldwide. Many STIs do not show symptoms in their early stages, making regular screening an essential part of preventive healthcare. Early diagnosis allows timely treatment, reduces complications and helps prevent transmission to others.

Common STIs that can be detected

  • Chlamydia
  • Gonorrhoea
  • Syphilis
  • HIV
  • Human papillomavirus (HPV)
  • Herpes simplex virus (HSV)
  • Hepatitis B and C
  • Trichomoniasis
  • Mycoplasma genitalium

Why choose molecular STI testing?

PCR-based molecular testing achieves high accuracy by detecting the DNA or RNA of infectious organisms. It provides:

  • High sensitivity and specificity
  • Results that allow timely treatment
  • Early detection before symptoms appear
  • Fewer false-negative results

Who should get tested?

Routine STI screening is recommended for sexually active individuals, people with new or multiple partners, pregnant women, and anyone experiencing symptoms such as unusual discharge, pain or sores.

Protect your health today

Regular STI testing protects both your health and your partner's. Early diagnosis means earlier treatment, fewer complications and better long-term outcomes.
